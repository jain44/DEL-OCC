AIRPORT OPERATIONS CONTROL CENTER
1. Challenge Brief
Build a real-time Airport Operations Control Center that transforms the provided multi-table airport 
dataset into a unified, interactive operational interface for monitoring flights, gates, baggage, 
passengers, security, maintenance, staff and other airport activities.
2. Background
Modern airports operate as complex, time-sensitive ecosystems in which multiple departments must 
coordinate simultaneously. Operational teams need a single interface that can convert fragmented data 
into clear situational awareness, highlight disruptions, and support rapid operational decisions.
3. Your Mission
Design and develop a production-quality frontend that behaves like an Airport Operations Control 
Center. The application should unify the supplied datasets, expose meaningful relationships between 
them, and simulate a live operational environment rather than presenting a collection of static charts.

6. Functional Standard
A visually attractive dashboard alone is not sufficient. The final solution must demonstrate meaningful 
functionality and operational thinking. Participants should decide how the supplied data can be 
transformed into a coherent workflow for airport operators and how users can move from high-level 
monitoring to detailed investigation.
7. Real-Time Simulation
The provided dataset may be static, but the application must create the experience of a changing 
operational environment. Teams may simulate event progression, status changes, alerts, queue 
updates, timelines or other live behaviours using frontend logic. The simulation should remain 
consistent with the supplied data and the product experience.
